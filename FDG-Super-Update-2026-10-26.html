Weekly AI briefing
FDG Super Update
Week of 19 to 26 October 2026 (NZ time)

This was a busy week for agents and enterprise controls. There were no new frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic or xAI. The main changes were proactive "always-on" assistants, more ways to buy AI models, and admin controls catching up with how much staff use these tools.

Top 5 this week

01Anthropic launched Claude Duet (research preview). It is a proactive agent that sends a daily brief, checks connected apps every few hours and runs tasks in the background. It asks for approval before sending, paying or deleting anything. For now only US Pro and Max users can join the waitlist, but it shows where business assistants are going. Source
02Claude chat and Cowork merged into one experience for Enterprise plans. Admins also get new cost controls: an "Efficient mode" that keeps users on the cheaper Sonnet and Haiku models, spend limits and pooled budgets. Source
03OpenAI and Anthropic models are now sold through Google Cloud. OpenAI's models are on request in Google's Model Garden, with general availability "in the coming weeks". The full Claude Platform is generally available on Google Cloud. Both can be paid for from existing Google Cloud commitments. OpenAI · Anthropic
04Gemini Notebook now works from inside the Gemini app for all Google Workspace customers. You can add sources, save notes and ask questions about a notebook from a normal Gemini chat. This is a simple way to build a searchable technical library. Source
05Copilot in OneDrive now has reusable "skills" and can work across many files (preview). It needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence, with general availability planned for late December. From mid-November, creating an agent in OneDrive or SharePoint will open Copilot Agent Builder. Source

AI at FDG

Finance

  • Monthly variance commentary. Use Copilot in Excel (Finance in M365 Copilot) or Claude for Excel on the margin report by product family (seismic, clamps, MODFRAME). First step: run the prompt "Show per-period variances exceeding 10% and summarise the drivers" on last month's report, then compare the output with your own commentary. Source
  • Supplier statement reconciliation. Have Copilot match supplier statements against the AP ledger and flag unmatched items, as a scheduled report. First step: pick the three suppliers with the most invoices and test one month. Source

Technical

  • Technical query library. Load FDG test reports, product data sheets and approved installation guides into a Gemini Notebook, or a Claude or ChatGPT project. Staff can then ask it questions and get cited answers to draft replies, which an engineer still reviews. First step: build the notebook for one product range and test it against ten queries you have already answered. Source
  • Audit calculation workbooks. Claude for Excel has a model-auditing skill that can review bracing calculation spreadsheets for broken references, hard-coded values and unit mismatches. First step: run it on a copy of one calculation workbook and log what it finds. Source

Marketing

  • One case study, four formats. Rewrite a single project case study or technical article for the web, email, LinkedIn and a landing page, with a section answering common objections. First step: do this with your most recent MODFRAME project write-up. Source
  • CPD material. Gemini Notebook now gives step-by-step control over the slide decks and video overviews it generates. Use it to draft CPD slides from FDG's seismic restraint guidance, then have an engineer check them. First step: create one 10-slide draft. Source

Operations

  • Exception handling and standard operating procedures. Turn a description of how backorders, damaged goods or short shipments are handled today into a decision flow with triggers, owners and escalation rules. First step: record the warehouse lead walking through one exception and have ChatGPT or Claude draft the procedure from the transcript. Source
  • Supplier performance reviews. Summarise lead-time and delivery data for each overseas supplier, flag missed agreements and list the questions for the next review. First step: export 12 months of purchase-order promised dates against received dates for your top five suppliers. Source

Customer service

  • Inbox triage. Sort incoming email into quote, return, delivery and billing queues, with a draft reply for each that staff review. As Distribution Strategy Group points out, a usable quote still needs the right customer price, unit of measure, freight terms and delivery commitment. First step: classify one week of shared-inbox emails and check the accuracy. Source
  • After-hours first-line answers. HubSpot reports its Customer Agent resolves about 70% of support conversations on its own, and it is mainly used for after-hours cover and simple first-line queries. First step: start the 28-day trial on product-document and order-status questions only. Source

Sales

  • Engineer and project research. Before visits to consulting engineers, build one-page briefs on the firm, its current projects and points to raise about specifying FDG. First step: create a reusable prompt or Claude project and test it on your next three meetings. Source
  • Call notes into HubSpot. Convert call notes into structured CRM updates and follow-up emails, and build a reusable answer library for tender questionnaires. First step: ask reps to paste notes from one call a day for a week and measure how complete the CRM is. Source

Trends and best practice

  • Redesign the workflow, not just the task. Nearly three-quarters of AI "high performers" have fundamentally redesigned workflows around AI, compared with about a quarter of other companies. Only 37% of all respondents see any EBIT impact, even though 80% report individual productivity gains. Source
  • Agents are growing faster than the controls around them. BCG says agents' share of AI value rose from 17% to 22%, but only 5% of companies have the full set of critical controls in place. Source
  • Keep agents simple. Anthropic recommends one agent loop with skills before splitting work across several agents, and checking results against a small test set. Skills are now an open standard. Guidance · Open standard
  • Set cost and audit controls before a wide rollout. This week's releases include per-user quota limits in Gemini Enterprise, Efficient mode and spend limits in Claude, and Vault exports of Gemini conversations. Agent-style work uses far more tokens than chat. Google · Anthropic

Try this week

  1. Build a Gemini Notebook, or a Claude or ChatGPT project, for one product range and test it against ten past technical queries.
  2. Ask finance to run the 10% variance prompt on last month's margin report and compare the result with their own commentary.
  3. Export one week of the customer-service inbox, with personal details removed, and test how accurately AI sorts it into quote, return, delivery and billing.
