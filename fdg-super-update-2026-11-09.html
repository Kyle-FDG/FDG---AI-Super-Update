FDG Super Update
Week of 2 to 9 November 2026 (NZ time)

Hi Kyle. A quiet week for new frontier models and a busy one for business controls. Anthropic and Microsoft shipped admin and governance features, OpenAI made its always-on dots agents generally available on enterprise plans, and McKinsey's latest survey shows productivity gains are still not turning into profit for most firms. We found no reports of a major multi-provider outage.

Top 5 this week

01Anthropic tightened Claude Enterprise controls (5 Nov). A new security centre compares your settings with Anthropic's recommended configuration, and prompt and tool-call hooks are now generally available so data-loss tools can check what staff send. Source
02OpenAI's always-on dots are generally available on ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu (4 Nov). Dots keep working on tasks between conversations. Admins have a preview period until 18 Nov, when dots switch on by default (reported by Releasebot from OpenAI's release notes). Source
03Microsoft 365 Copilot picked up practical updates (4 Nov). Researcher can now use the HubSpot connector, Excel chat answers link to the cells Copilot changed, and Copilot Cowork can complete web steps in your own Edge browser. Source
04McKinsey's 2026 State of AI: productivity up, profit mostly not (reported 4 Nov). About 80% of respondents report productivity gains but only 37% see any positive EBIT effect, and large firms are scaling agents faster than smaller ones (40% versus 22%). Source
05Gemini now analyses Google Forms responses (5 Nov). It runs the numbers on survey answers and suggests next steps, a useful pattern for customer and training feedback whichever office suite you use. Source

AI at FDG

Finance

  • Supplier statement reconciliation. Excel's Financial Reconciliation agent matches two tables, suggests matching rules and sorts lines into matched, possibly matched and unmatched, with a summary of fixes. It suits AP statement checks and bank recs. First step: run last month's largest supplier statement against the AP ledger export and compare with the manual result. Source
  • Margin analysis by product family. OpenAI's use-case guide shows finance teams using ChatGPT to write Python that automates parts of month-end, including reconciling millions of spreadsheet rows. Use ChatGPT or Claude data analysis to build a repeatable margin report for bracing, clamps, cable management and MODFRAME. First step: upload an anonymised quarter of sales lines and ask for a margin breakdown plus the script that produced it. Source

Technical

  • Answers from approved technical documents only. Copilot Search can now treat up to 100 SharePoint sites as authoritative, so answers draw on current test reports, install guides and design tables rather than old drafts. First step: list the SharePoint libraries that hold approved technical content and ask IT to mark them as authoritative. Source
  • Product data clean-up. Lowe's raised product tagging accuracy by 20% by applying AI to messy product data. Use Claude or ChatGPT to normalise load ratings, rod sizes, finishes and codes into one schema for the website, catalogues and BIM objects. First step: export 50 SKUs, prompt for a fixed attribute table, and check the results against datasheets. Source

Marketing

  • First-draft campaign emails. Promega saved 135 hours in six months by having AI write first drafts of email campaigns. Set up a Claude Project or custom GPT loaded with FDG's style guide and product facts for CPD invitations and launch emails in HubSpot. First step: draft next month's CPD invitation with it and track how long editing takes. Source
  • Case study decks in the FDG template. Copilot in PowerPoint can now take the theme from an attached deck, so project notes and photos become an on-brand case study draft. First step: attach the FDG master deck and one completed project's notes and ask for a five-slide case study. Source

Operations

  • Supplier backorder chasing. GE Appliances reports a 25% cut in backorders using a supplier collaboration agent across 600+ suppliers. A lighter version for FDG: AI reviews the open purchase order report each week, flags late lines and drafts chase emails. First step: paste this week's overdue PO export into Copilot or ChatGPT and ask for a prioritised chase list by supplier. Source
  • Project and NPD tracking with approval checkpoints. monday.com's guidance separates assistants that wait for prompts from agents that act on triggers, and recommends human approval steps and audit logs. First step: on one monday.com board, set an AI agent to flag overdue items weekly, with a person approving any update that goes out. Source

Customer service

  • Order and product queries through HubSpot's Customer agent. HubSpot's Agent Hub manages its built-in agents, including a Customer agent that answers from your knowledge base (Professional or Enterprise plans plus HubSpot Credits). First step: list the 20 most common order and product-selection questions and check whether the knowledge base answers each one. Source

For FDG, have Copilot in Outlook or a Claude Project draft replies to stock, lead-time and product-selection emails for staff to check and send. First step: collect ten recent queries and the replies sent, to use as examples in the prompt. Source

Sales

  • Account research before engineer meetings. Microsoft 365 Copilot Researcher can now use the HubSpot connector, combining CRM history with web research on a consultancy or contractor. First step: run Researcher on one consulting engineer before your next specification meeting and note what it gets wrong. Source

Trends and best practice

  • Productivity is not yet profit. McKinsey finds most firms report productivity gains but few see EBIT impact. The difference is redesigning workflows rather than speeding up existing tasks, and some time saved is lost to checking AI output. Diginomica · TNW
  • Adoption is a change programme. KOHLER reached 98% Copilot adoption in six weeks with 200+ champions and tiered training. AGCO had 900 leaders volunteer to build agents and cut some quality reviews from weeks to about an hour. KOHLER · AGCO
  • Governance is moving into admin settings. Security centres, prompt hooks and spend tags in Claude, and new Copilot defaults such as agent blueprints and usage-based billing, mean IT needs a regular review of AI settings, not a one-off policy. Anthropic · ChangePilot
  • Always-on agents need guardrails. Put approval checkpoints and audit logs in place before letting agents act on triggers, and expect their costs and limits to change. Source
