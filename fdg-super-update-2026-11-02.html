Weekly AI briefing
FDG Super Update
Week of 26 October to 2 November 2026 (NZ time)

It was a fairly busy week for models: OpenAI released one and Google announced one. There were also plan and admin changes from Anthropic, and some useful Google Workspace features. Microsoft and xAI were quiet, and I found no major outages.

Top 5 this week

01OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra. It is a faster, cheaper mid-sized model that can use a computer and act across apps. It's available in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API, and costs about half as much as the model it replaces. Source
02Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon. Google reports big gains in coding and agent tasks. For now only cyber-security defenders have access, and wider release is "coming soon". Source
03Anthropic renamed its Claude personal plans. Max 5x is now "Max" at US$100 a month, and Max 20x is now "Ultra" at US$200 a month. Verified Ultra accounts no longer have the five-hour usage cap. Source
04The Gemini app can now create, preview and edit files in the chat. This covers documents, spreadsheets, slide decks and PDFs, and decks can be downloaded as PPTX. Source
05For FDG: build a technical-query assistant from your own experts' knowledge. The engineering firm WSP is testing an AI system with about 300 engineers and scientists. It gives instant answers to technical questions based on senior specialists' knowledge. FDG's technical support could use the same pattern (see AI at FDG). Source

AI news

OpenAI

  • 26 Oct: GPT-6.1 Terra released in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. OpenAI reports fewer errors on its internal test set: at low reasoning effort the error rate dropped from 53.6% to 16.7%. Source
  • 27–29 Oct: ChatGPT plan and connector changes. Pro 100, Pro 500 and Business Premium seats now get twice their previous Work and Codex allowance, and a planned cut to Pro 200 was cancelled. iCloud Mail and Calendar can now connect to ChatGPT. Enterprise and Edu workspaces no longer switch automatically from Instant to higher thinking levels. Source

Anthropic

  • 28 Oct: New Claude Enterprise admin features. Smart reports, in-app surveys and pooled group budgets are now generally available. Managers can see what a team produced with Claude, what it cost, and where workflows broke down. Source
  • 30 Oct (US time): Personal plans renamed Max and Ultra (see Top 5). Prices and weekly limits are unchanged. The article doesn't say whether Team or Enterprise plans are affected. Source
  • 28–30 Oct: API changes. A new Usage and Cost Analytics API is in beta, the maximum request size rose from 32 MB to 128 MB, and fast mode for Claude Opus 4.8 will be removed on 30 November. Source

Google

  • 28 Oct: Gemini 4 Carbon announced. Introductory API pricing is US$2 per million input tokens and US$10 per million output tokens. Cyber defenders get access first. Source
  • 28 Oct: Fill with Gemini in Sheets now cites a web source for each cell, and one request can cover up to 3,000 cells. It's on Business Standard and Plus and Enterprise plans, rolling out now. Source
  • 29 Oct: The Gemini app can create and edit files in a chat (see Top 5). Source
  • 30 Oct: Gemini in Google Slides can edit a whole deck from one prompt, for example restyling or condensing it. Source

xAI (Grok)

  • 27 Oct: New moderation controls in the Grok API. Developers can now set how Grok handles NSFW, violence and profanity. Source
  • No new Grok models this week.

Meta

  • 30 Oct: Muse launched in Singapore, its first announced Asia-Pacific market. Muse is Meta's personal agent, and it now connects to services such as Grab, Shopee and Singapore Airlines. It asks for approval before sending email, making purchases or initiating payments. Source

Microsoft Copilot

  • 27 Oct: Small updates for everyday users. Copilot settings can now be opened from classic Outlook for Windows (File > Copilot settings), and Copilot Chat can now summarise a recorded call in the Teams Queues app. Otherwise it was quiet. Source

Always-on agents

  • OpenAI Dots (28 Oct): you can now text your ChatGPT "dot", OpenAI's persistent assistant, by iMessage or RCS. Texting is in beta for US Pro users only, and when the dot needs approval it sends a link to ChatGPT. Source
  • Meta Muse (30 Oct): expanded to Singapore (see Meta). Source
  • Grok Bot: Nothing new this week.

Other notable

  • I found no major cross-provider outages in the window.

AI at FDG

Finance

  • Vendor spend and duplicate check. Upload an accounts payable export to a business-tier ChatGPT or Claude account. Ask for the top 10 suppliers by spend, duplicate supplier names and options to consolidate. First step: export 12 months of AP lines from the finance system with bank details removed, and run that prompt. Source
  • Sales and margin forecast by product line. Use Copilot in Excel to clean the data and add rolling averages. Then use the Analyst agent to project revenue by category: bracing, clamps, cable management and MODFRAME. First step: follow Microsoft's six-step forecasting scenario on three years of sales history. Source

Technical

  • Technical query assistant. Use a Claude or ChatGPT project, or a Copilot Studio agent on SharePoint. Ground it in FDG test reports, seismic design guides and past answers to engineers. WSP is testing the same idea with about 300 engineers and scientists. First step: collect the 20 most common technical queries and the answers you sent. Load them with the source documents, then compare the AI's answers to yours. Source
  • First drafts of test reports. Give the Gemini app (or Claude) your report template and the raw test data, and have it draft the report. You can then edit in place and export it. First step: redo one completed report this way. An engineer then checks every number and claim against the data. Source

Marketing

  • Check how AI search engines describe FDG. Consulting engineers increasingly ask ChatGPT and Gemini for product recommendations. HubSpot AEO (included in Marketing Hub Pro and Enterprise) tracks brand visibility, competitor share of voice and which sources the AI cites. First step: run 10 questions an engineer might ask, such as "seismic bracing suppliers NZ" or "modular framing for MEP", in ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, and record where FDG appears. Source
  • Refresh CPD and sales decks in one pass. Gemini in Slides can now restyle or condense a whole deck from one prompt, and the changes can be undone. First step: pick one older CPD deck and ask it to condense the deck to 20 slides and apply your current template. Check that Copilot in PowerPoint can do the same if FDG works mainly in Microsoft 365. Source

Operations

  • Replenishment planning. Microsoft's sample replenishment agent is a useful model. It reads ERP and logistics data, forecasts demand, flags slow movers and drafts orders for a person to approve. First step: don't build the agent yet. In Excel with Copilot, analyse 24 months of sales by SKU to flag stockouts and slow movers, and compare the results with current reorder points. Source
  • Meeting recaps and action tracking. John Holland project managers use Teams Copilot recaps to capture actions and open questions from client and tender meetings. First step: turn on Teams recap for the weekly operations meeting, and copy the actions into the relevant monday.com board. Source

Customer service

  • First-line answers from the HubSpot customer agent. It responds to support conversations, so its answers will only be as good as FDG's knowledge base. First step: write 15 short knowledge base articles on lead times, ordering, returns and basic product selection before you switch the agent on. Source
  • Faster follow-up on phone queries. If FDG's phone lines run on Teams call queues, Copilot Chat can now summarise a recorded queue call, so staff don't need to replay it. First step: test it on a week of recorded calls and check the summaries before using them in quotes. Source

Sales

  • Specification business development with the HubSpot prospecting agent. You set up "plays" for each target market. The agent finds companies showing buying signals, suggests contacts and drafts outreach, and you can review every email before it's sent. First step: create one play aimed at consulting engineers, with review-before-send turned on. Source
  • Account and project research with sources. Fill with Gemini can now complete research columns in Sheets and cite the web source for each cell. First step: list 50 target engineering and MEP firms, ask for recent NZ projects and office locations, and spot-check the cited sources. Source

Trends and best practice

  • Redesign the workflow, not just the tool. BCG surveyed 11,749 workers. Companies that redesign workflows were 24 points more likely to see measurable business improvement. Clear strategy added about 25 points, against about 5 points for better tools alone. 66% of frontline users get limited or no guidance on how to use the time they save. Source
  • Agents are arriving inside the apps you already pay for. Gartner predicted that 40% of enterprise apps would include task-specific agents by 2026, up from less than 5% in 2025. HubSpot and Microsoft are following that path, so check what is in your current licences before buying new tools. Source
  • Measure value and set controls. Vendors are adding tools to measure value and control spending, such as Claude's smart reports and pooled budgets. Agree what "value" means for each team before rolling AI out widely. Source
  • Require sources and keep a human check. Models make fewer errors than before but still make some: GPT-6.1 Terra is still wrong on 16.7% of OpenAI's test prompts at low effort. For engineering and pricing work, ask for cited sources and have someone check the figures. Source
